From Aid to Ability Foundation

Moving communities from aid dependency to sustainable self-reliance

FAAF is a non-profit, community-based organization in Malawi that transforms the development paradigm by investing in human capital — skills development, mentorship, and economic opportunity creation.

Our Founder

Boaz Selemani — transforming lived experience into lasting change

Boaz founded FAAF in 2020 at age 20. Born in the DRC and raised in Dzaleka Refugee Camp, he experienced aid dependency firsthand and refused to accept it as permanent reality. Today he leads FAAF through servant leadership, building systems that outlast individual involvement.

The Problem

Malawi's development paradox

Despite billions in international assistance, Malawi remains one of the world's poorest nations. The missing link is not resources — it's relational infrastructure: skills, mentorship, and economic opportunities that convert potential into productivity.

What We Do

Five integrated program pillars

Pillar 1

Skills Development

Market-responsive technical and soft skills training that converts unemployed individuals into productive workers and entrepreneurs.

Pillar 2

Mentorship Programs

Relational infrastructure that accelerates growth, prevents costly mistakes, and transmits values alongside skills.

Pillar 3

Entrepreneurship & Economic Empowerment

Converting skills into sustainable enterprises through capital, coaching, and market access.

Pillar 4

Youth & Child Empowerment

Investing early to prevent dependency before it takes root through leadership academies and child protection.

Pillar 5

Community Development & Institutional Strengthening

Building community capacity to manage its own development trajectory with governance training and knowledge exchange.
