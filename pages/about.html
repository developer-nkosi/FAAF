Who We Are

Our Story

FAAF is a non-profit, community-based organization in Malawi that transforms the development paradigm by investing in human capital, skills development, mentorship, and economic opportunity creation.

Our Founder

Boaz Selemani transforming lived experience into lasting change

Boaz founded FAAF in 2020 at age 20. Born in the DRC and raised in Dzaleka Refugee Camp, he experienced aid dependency firsthand and refused to accept it as permanent reality. Today he leads FAAF through servant leadership, building systems that outlast individual involvement.

Our Approach

From aid to ability

FAAF works by combining skills, mentorship, opportunity, and local leadership. We do not only deliver one-off support; we build systems that help people move from dependency to agency.

1. Discover

We listen to the realities, gaps, and aspirations in communities before designing interventions.

2. Build

We create practical pathways for livelihoods, mentorship, and leadership development.

3. Sustain

We strengthen local structures so progress continues long after the initial initiative ends.
