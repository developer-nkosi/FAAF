Our Team

Committed leaders and advocates

FAAF is guided by a team of committed leaders, community advocates, and young professionals who contribute their knowledge, creativity, and dedication to advancing our mission. Through servant leadership, teamwork, and accountability, we work together to design and implement initiatives that respond to community needs while strengthening local capacity.

Our team is built on the belief that sustainable change does not come from one individual — it comes from empowered people working together. We value collaboration, inclusiveness, innovation, and community ownership as the foundation of everything we do.

Governance

Accountability and transparency

FAAF is committed to the highest standards of organizational governance and accountability.

Board of Directors

5-7 members providing strategic oversight, fiduciary responsibility, and mission guardianship. Independent majority required.

Annual Independent Audit

Financial statements audited by a registered firm; publicly available summary.

Beneficiary Feedback

Anonymous channels for program complaints and suggestions; quarterly satisfaction surveys.

Child Safeguarding

Designated safeguarding officer; mandatory reporting protocols; background checks for all staff and mentors.

Conflict of Interest

Annual declarations; recusal protocols; transparency in procurement and hiring.

Data Protection

Participant consent protocols; restricted data access; no unauthorized sharing with donors or media.
