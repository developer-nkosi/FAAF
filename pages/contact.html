Call to Action

FAAF is seeking partners in transformation

The transition from aid to ability is not automatic. It requires intentional design, disciplined execution, and unwavering commitment to human dignity. FAAF exists to make that transition possible one individual, one community, one generation at a time.

For Funders

Invest in a model designed to generate returns in human capability and community resilience.

For Mentors

Share your experience and guide the next generation of Malawian leaders.

For Employers

Access a pipeline of skilled, motivated, and mentored workers.

For Communities

Partner with us to design programs that reflect your realities and aspirations.

For Advocates

Amplify the message that development must be measured by independence created, not dependence sustained.

Current Status

Institution-Building Phase (2026–2027)

FAAF is currently in the institution-building phase, with the following immediate priorities:

Priority Focus Area
1 Legal Registration --> Complete non-profit registration and tax-exempt status in Malawi
2 Governance Structure --> Constitute Board of Directors; finalize Constitution
3 Team Building --> Recruit core staff (Programs Director, MEL Officer, Finance Manager)
4 Pilot Program --> Launch integrated skills and mentorship pilot serving 100 youth
5 Resource Mobilization --> Secure initial funding from foundations, individual donors, and corporate partners
6 Systems Development --> Establish financial management, MEL, communications, and HR systems
