Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy describes how From Aid to Ability Foundation collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Who we are

1. Purpose of this policy

From Aid to Ability Foundation (FAAF) is committed to protecting the privacy and dignity of the communities, partners, and supporters we serve. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information in connection with our work in Malawi.

Information We Collect

2. Information we collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, organization details, program participation data, interest in volunteering, partnership information, and communications you send to us. We also collect limited technical information such as browser details to improve website functionality and security.

How We Use Information

3. How we use information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, deliver programs, coordinate mentoring and support, assess impact, communicate opportunities, and improve our services. We do not sell personal data. We may share information only where necessary to support the work of FAAF or comply with legal obligations.

Data Protection

4. Data protection and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. Information is retained only for as long as needed for program delivery, reporting, legal compliance, or legitimate organizational purposes.

Your Rights

5. Your rights

You may request access to your information, request corrections, ask for deletion where lawful, or object to certain uses of your data. If you would like to exercise these rights, please contact us and we will respond in line with applicable law and our internal process.

Partnerships

6. Third-party and partner information

We may work with community organizations, local institutions, volunteers, and donors. In these cases, information may be shared only when required to support partnership coordination, program delivery, or safeguarding responsibilities and only under appropriate confidentiality standards.

Contact

Questions?

If you have questions about this privacy policy, need help understanding how your information is used, or want to request a change, contact FAAF directly.
