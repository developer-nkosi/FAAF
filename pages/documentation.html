About FAAF

How we work

FAAF exists to move families and communities in Malawi from dependency to capability. Our work is built around practical participation: people gain skills, meet mentors, build income streams, and strengthen local systems that can continue without heavy external support.

Mission-first approach

We align every program to long-term community resilience, not short-term aid cycles. Our goal is sustainable self-reliance, not dependence.

Human capability

We focus on skills, confidence, mentorship, and opportunity creation so people can become productive contributors to their own societies.

Partnership model

We work with partners, institutions, donors, and community leaders to design locally relevant solutions and increase impact at scale.
